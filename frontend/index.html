The House

A home that feels like it was waiting for you.

Set behind a gated garden in the residential quiet of Ilboru, Toiwo Residence offers privacy, comfort, and the kind of calm that makes stays feel longer than they are.

Arched doorways, warm wood details, and a shaded courtyard create a home that is both elegant and easygoing. Four bedrooms, generous living spaces, and a terrace for outdoor dining make it ideal for families, couples, and safari groups.
